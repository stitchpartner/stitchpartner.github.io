Best Cloth Cutting Scissors for Tailors: How to Choose 12 Inch Shears

By Aashu, owner of Stitch Partner, a store for tailoring tools and sewing machine spare parts in India.

Why 12 inch shears are the tailor's standard

Long blades let you cut a long, smooth line in one stroke, which gives cleaner edges on large fabric spreads than short scissors that need many small snips.

What to look for

  • Blade steel: hardened or high-carbon steel holds an edge longer on cotton, denim and synthetics.
  • Bent handle: an offset handle keeps the lower blade flat on the table so the fabric does not lift.
  • Grip: a comfortable, non-slip handle matters when you cut all day.
  • Pivot: the blades should close smoothly along the full length without a gap.

Keep them sharp

Use fabric shears only on fabric, because paper dulls the edge quickly. Wipe the blades after use, put a drop of machine oil on the pivot now and then, and have a professional sharpen them when the cut starts to chew the fabric.

Which size for which work

SizeBest for
8 inchSmall trimming, detail cutting, light fabric
10 inchGeneral tailoring and alteration work
12 inchLong straight cuts, suit and uniform cloth, daily tailoring

Longer blades make long cuts with fewer stops, which gives a cleaner edge. Shorter blades give more control on curves.

Common problems and fixes

  • Scissors chew the fabric: the blades are dull, or the pivot screw is loose. Tighten the screw lightly and have the blades sharpened.
  • Cuts well only near the tip or the heel: the blades are not meeting evenly. Check the pivot and sharpen both blades.
  • Hand or wrist pain: the handle size does not fit your hand, or you are cutting for hours. Use a handle that suits your grip, or consider electric fabric scissors for long cutting jobs.

Care that keeps them cutting

  • Use fabric shears for fabric only. Cutting paper or thread dulls them quickly.
  • Wipe the blades after use and keep them dry.
  • Put a drop of oil on the pivot now and then.
  • Store them closed in a sheath or a drawer.

Frequently asked questions

How often should I sharpen tailoring scissors?

Sharpen them when the cut quality drops: the blades start to chew, skip or leave a ragged edge. How often depends on how much and what kind of fabric you cut.

Is a 12 inch pair better than a 10 inch pair?

For long straight cuts, yes. For detailed or curved cutting, a shorter pair gives more control. Many tailors keep both sizes.

Can I use fabric scissors to cut paper?

It is best not to. Paper wears the blade edge quickly and makes fabric cutting worse afterwards.

Are electric scissors better than manual shears?

Electric scissors reduce hand strain on long cutting jobs, while manual shears give more control on curves and cost less to maintain. Choose by the work you do most.
