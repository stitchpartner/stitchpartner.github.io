Siruba 747 Overlock Machine Spare Parts: What to Stock

By Aashu, owner of Stitch Partner, a store for overlock machine spare parts in India.

Parts that wear first

On a busy overlock line, the needle plate, loopers, thread guides and cover hinges take the most wear. Skipped stitches, cut threads and loose covers usually point to one of these parts.

How to order the right part

  • Use the part code: the code stamped or printed on the old part is the safest match.
  • Note the machine model: for example Siruba 747 with 4 threads.
  • Compare before fitting: check the old and new parts side by side.
