Parts that wear first

On a busy overlock line, the needle plate, loopers, thread guides and cover hinges take the most wear. Skipped stitches, cut threads and loose covers usually point to one of these parts.

How to order the right part

  • Use the part code: the code stamped or printed on the old part is the safest match.
  • Note the machine model: for example Siruba 747 with 4 threads.
  • Compare before fitting: check the old and new parts side by side.

Daily and weekly care

  • Oil the machine as the manual shows, and clean lint from the looper area every day.
  • Check the needle at the start of each shift and change it when it is blunt or bent.
  • Look at the knives each week. A dull knife shows first as a ragged edge.
  • Do not force fabric. Let the feed dog move it to protect the needle and looper.

Frequently asked questions

Which parts wear out first on an overlock machine?

Needles, loopers, knives and feed dogs wear first because they work against fabric all day. Keep spares of these on hand.

How do I find the correct needle plate for my overlock?

Use your exact machine model and the parts book part number. Plates look alike but hole size and shape differ between versions. Compare numbers in the needle plate and feed dog chart, and send a photo if unsure.

Why does my overlock skip stitches?

Start with the needle: a bent, blunt or wrong needle is the usual cause. If a new needle does not help, the looper timing or needle height may need adjustment by a mechanic.

Can I use parts from another brand on my Siruba?

Do not assume so. Some parts are interchangeable and many are not. Always compare part numbers or confirm with the supplier before ordering.
