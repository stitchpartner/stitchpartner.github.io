Industrial Steam Iron with Boiler: Buying and Spare Parts Guide

By Aashu, owner of Stitch Partner, a store for steam iron parts and sewing machine spare parts in India.

Two common types

A boiler steam iron has a separate tank that keeps producing steam while you press, which suits continuous work in a garment unit. A gravity feed iron uses a raised water tank and suits a smaller tailor shop or lighter daily use.

What to check before buying

  • Power rating and boiler size: bigger boilers run longer between refills.
  • Water supply: tank fill or pump fed, depending on how you work.
  • Table setup: a vacuum ironing table needs a suitable foam and cover.

Spare parts worth keeping

Water pumps, safety caps, seal washers, hoses, contactors, non-return valves and table foam and cloth wear with regular use. Always use a safety cap made for your boiler and follow the maker's instructions when opening a hot boiler.

Common problems and fixes

  • No steam or weak steam: scale in the tank or nozzle, low water, or a blocked hose. Descale, clean the nozzle and check the water level.
  • Spitting water: temperature set too low for steam, or scale. Raise the temperature and descale.
  • Leaking: a worn gasket or a loose hose connection. Replace the gasket and tighten the connection.
  • Not heating: a faulty thermostat or heating element, or a blown fuse. Unplug it and have the element and thermostat tested.

Daily care

  • Use clean, low-mineral water where you can. Hard water builds scale quickly.
  • Empty the tank at the end of the day.
  • Descale on a regular schedule, more often if your water is hard.
  • Always unplug and let the iron cool before opening the tank.

Frequently asked questions

Should I buy a bottle iron or a boiler iron?

Choose a bottle iron for light daily use in a boutique or tailoring shop. Choose a boiler iron when you press for many hours a day and want longer runs between refills.

How often should I descale a steam iron?

It depends on your water. In hard water areas, descale often; in soft water areas, less often. Weak steam or white flakes are signs it is due.

Why does my steam iron spit water?

Usually the temperature is too low for steam, or scale is blocking the nozzle. Raise the temperature and descale the iron.

Can I use tap water in an industrial steam iron?

You can if the water is soft, but hard tap water causes scale that shortens the life of the heating element and nozzle. Clean or low-mineral water is safer.
