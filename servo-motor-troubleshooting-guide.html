Sewing Machine Servo Motor Problems: Not Starting, Humming, Running by Itself

Short answer: most servo motor faults are not a dead motor. Turn the hand wheel, measure the socket voltage, reseat every plug, then restart. If the motor turns freely by hand and the fault returns at once, the control board, encoder or pedal is the part to replace.

Symptom and first fix

Safety

Switch off and wait about five minutes before opening a control box, because capacitors hold a charge. Check plugs yourself; leave boards to a technician.
