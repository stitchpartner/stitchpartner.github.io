110mm vs 125mm Cloth Cutting Machine: Which One to Buy

By Aashu, owner of Stitch Partner, a store for cutting machines and sewing machine spare parts in India.

What the number means

The 110mm or 125mm figure is the size of the round blade. A larger blade generally cuts through a taller stack of fabric, while a smaller one is lighter and easier to steer around curves. Choose by the thickness of the lay you cut most often.

How to decide

  • Small stacks and curved patterns: a 110mm machine is easier to handle.
  • Tall lays of fabric: a 125mm machine gives more cutting depth.
  • Daily heavy use: look at a servo motor model, which runs cooler and quieter.

Parts that wear out first

Blades, the base plate, handle, wheels, switch and worm gear see the most use. Keep the blade sharp, oil the moving parts as the maker advises, and replace a worn gear before it damages the motor.

Choosing the blade size

A larger blade diameter cuts a thicker stack of fabric, but it is heavier and needs more power. Use a 110mm machine for light to medium stacks and small tailoring or boutique work, and a 125mm machine when you regularly cut taller stacks. Always check the maximum cutting thickness in the specification of your exact model, because it differs between brands.

Safety and maintenance

  • Unplug the machine before changing a blade or cleaning.
  • Keep the blade guard in place and wear a cut-resistant glove on the holding hand.
  • Wipe lint from the base plate and blade area after each shift.
  • Sharpen only when the cut quality drops; over-sharpening wears the blade quickly.

Frequently asked questions

Should I buy a 110mm or a 125mm cloth cutting machine?

Choose by the height of the fabric stack you cut most often. 110mm suits lighter work. 125mm suits taller stacks. Check the maximum cutting thickness in the model specification.

How often should the blade be changed?

There is no fixed interval. Change or sharpen the blade when edges turn ragged, the motor strains, or you see chips on the blade edge.

Why does the machine cut a ragged edge?

The most common reason is a dull or chipped blade. Check sharpness first, then blade bolt tightness and the base plate.

Can carbon brushes be replaced?

Yes. Carbon brushes are wear parts. Replace them as a pair when the motor loses power or sparks heavily.
